Questions people actually ask

Is this a tout service?

No. A tout shows you the winners. This shows you every prediction it made, including the ones it lost, and the calibration curve that says whether "70%" has actually meant 70%. If the model has a cold month the app tells you so on the paywall itself.

Will it make me money?

Nobody can promise that and anybody who does is selling something else. Break-even at -110 is 52.4%, and beating it consistently is hard. What this gives you is a number to compare the market's number against, and an honest record of how that comparison has gone.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources — play-by-play, rosters, depth charts, snap counts, injuries and scoreboards — rebuilt on a schedule by an automated pipeline. The whole dataset the app reads is published in the open.

Do I need an account?

No. It runs in the browser and remembers your settings on your device. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

Do I need two subscriptions for college and the NFL?

No. One app, one price, both leagues, at every tier including free. The seasons barely overlap, which is the whole reason they belong together rather than in two apps you pay for twice.

Can other people see my picks?

Only what you choose. Two separate switches: show your record, and show your picks. A private account hides the picks from everyone but your followers while your win/loss line can stay public — the proof without the plays. Or keep all of it to yourself.

How live are the scores?

The app polls the scoreboard itself every twenty seconds while a game is in progress, and pauses when it is in the background. The published dataset behind it rebuilds on a schedule for everything that is not a live score.
